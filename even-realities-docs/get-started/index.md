# Get Started

*From zero to a Hello-on-glasses app in 15 minutes - plus the deep-dive pages for each step.*

Source: https://hub.evenrealities.com/docs/get-started

> **Last updated:** 2026-06-11

Start here if you're new to Even Hub. The [Quickstart](/docs/get-started/quickstart/) page is the condensed one-page path; every linked deep dive expands the step it points to.

- **Without glasses yet?** Complete [Sign in](/docs/get-started/quickstart/sign-in), [Install Node.js & npm](/docs/get-started/quickstart/install-node), [Install Even Hub tooling](/docs/get-started/quickstart/install-tools), and [Your First App](/docs/get-started/quickstart/first-app) - all doable without hardware.
- **Have your Even G2?** Continue through [Hardware](/docs/get-started/quickstart/hardware) for pairing, firmware, Developer Mode, and firewall setup.
- **Want the big picture?** [Architecture](/docs/get-started/architecture) explains how your code reaches the glasses.
- **Want a head start?** [Templates](/docs/get-started/quickstart/templates) skips the manual scaffold with a complete starter project + first-party component library.
